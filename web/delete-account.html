Account & Data Deletion

Compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) & Google Play Data Safety Policy

Zero Server Note Storage: Prism is engineered as a private, local-first markdown notebook. We do not operate proprietary user accounts, centralized databases, or cloud servers storing your personal thoughts, notes, audio recordings, or metadata.

Method 1: In-App Instant Self-Service Reset Recommended

If you still have the Prism app installed on your device, you can instantly and irreversibly erase all local data and configurations:

  1. Open Prism on your iOS or Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete All Data & Reset.
  5. A confirmation modal will appear detailing the erasure. Type DELETE into the confirmation field and tap Delete Everything.
What happens during in-app reset: All local markdown notes, encrypted SQLite database indexes, audio attachments, application cache, and encryption keys stored in your device's Secure Storage are permanently deleted. Any active Google Drive OAuth tokens are revoked, RevenueCat anonymous subscriber identifiers are cleared, and the app resets to initial onboarding.

Method 2: Off-Device Deletion Request (DSAR) Web / Email

If you have uninstalled the Prism app, lost access to your device, or wish to request the deletion of associated non-note telemetry or support identifiers:

Under GDPR Article 17 ("Right to Erasure") and CCPA/CPRA § 1798.105, you may submit a formal Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) to our privacy team:

  1. Send an email to privacy@smallgiantstudio.com with the subject line [Data Deletion Request] - Prism.
  2. Include your approximate installation dates, platform (iOS or Android), and any support ticket reference numbers or anonymous installation ID if known.
  3. Our security and privacy team will verify the request and purge all associated server records (including Firebase crash diagnostics and RevenueCat subscriber profiles) within 30 days.

Method 3: Google Drive Cloud Sync Revocation Third-Party Sync

If you linked Prism to Google Drive for cloud synchronization, Prism connects directly between your device and Google APIs using restricted drive.appdata scope. Prism never receives or stores your Google login credentials.

To independently revoke Prism's access to your Google account at any time:

  1. Go to your Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Locate Prism under "Third-party apps with account access".
  3. Click Prism and select Remove Access.
  4. To delete existing app data files from Google Drive, open Google Drive on the web → Settings (Gear) → Manage Apps → find Prism → Options → Delete hidden app data.
